// FAQ

Common Questions

How fast can you turn around a repair?

Most services usually take between 1 and 7 days from drop-off to completion. Timing can be longer depending on parts availability or diagnostic complexity. We will let you know as soon as we identify a delay.

Do you give quotes before starting work?

Yes. We provide a written quote and only proceed once you approve it. The diagnostic fee is waived if you move forward with the repair.

Can I supply my own parts for a build?

Yes. We can assemble and test customer-supplied components. Labor pricing still applies.

What does bench testing include?

Stress testing under load, temperature and clock monitoring, memory validation, storage health checks, and a final run of your target software or games.

Do you offer warranties on labor?

Yes. All labor is covered for 30 days from pickup. Manufacturer warranties on parts remain with the manufacturer.
